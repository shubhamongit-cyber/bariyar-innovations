## UI Direction
Use white as the dominant background. Use blue for navigation, headings, links and primary buttons. Use orange for important CTAs and highlights. Use red sparingly for accents, alerts, badges and innovation details.

Avoid making the whole site dark. The intended look is a clean, premium, cool white interface with blue/orange energy.

## Logo
Use the supplied SVG starter logo on light backgrounds. Keep sufficient white space around it.
